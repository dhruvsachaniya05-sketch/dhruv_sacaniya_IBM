⚠️ The Crisis

Healthcare Breakdown in Rural India

Millions face life-threatening barriers to basic healthcare every single day — SwasthyaAI is built to change that.

🏥

Lack of Doctors

India has only 1 doctor per 1,457 rural patients — far below WHO's standard of 1 per 1,000.

🛣️

Long Distance

Patients travel 40–80 km to the nearest hospital, losing critical treatment hours every journey.

🗣️

Language Barriers

700+ tribal dialects go unsupported — most systems only speak Hindi or English.

💊

Medicine Shortage

Over 40% of rural PHCs face chronic shortages of essential medicines and vaccines.

⏰

Delayed Treatment

Late diagnosis causes preventable deaths — especially in maternal, child & emergency care.
